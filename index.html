How to crop a shipping label to 4×6

  1. Drop or paste your label

    Use the full-page PDF or screenshot you got from USPS, UPS, FedEx, Pirate Ship, eBay, Poshmark, Mercari or Depop. Multi-page PDF, PNG, JPG and WEBP files work, and Ctrl+V pastes straight from your clipboard.

  2. The label is found for you

    A vision model running in your browser scans every page, finds the shipping label and crops it to a 4.00 × 6.00 inch image (1200 × 1800 px, 300 DPI). If a PDF holds more than one label, pick the one you need from the candidates it shows.

  3. Rotate, print or download

    Rotate 90° or flip 180° if the label is sideways, then print it on your 4×6 thermal printer or save it as a PNG.

Questions

Is Crop This Label free?

Yes. There is no account to make and nothing to install.

Are my labels uploaded anywhere?

No. Cropping runs entirely in your browser with a local model, so your files and your customers' addresses never leave your device.

Which printers does it work with?

It makes a standard 4×6 inch image at 300 DPI, the size desktop thermal label printers take. You can also save the PNG and print it from any app.

My PDF has several pages. Will it find the label?

Yes. It scans every page and lists each label it finds with its page number and confidence, so packing slips and instructions are left out.

What if no label is found?

Make sure the barcode and address block are visible in the file, then try again with another PDF or screenshot.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in any modern desktop or mobile browser with JavaScript turned on.
